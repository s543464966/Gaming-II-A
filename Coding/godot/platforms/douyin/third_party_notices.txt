Douyin Godot runtime distribution

Official SDK documentation (1.0.4):
https://developer.open-douyin.com/docs/resource/zh-CN/mini-game/develop/guide/game-engine/godot/sdk-usage-guide

Official export template: web_release 4.5.1-rc-1.0.0.12
Official embedded launcher: 1.1.27, index.1954a1.js
Distribution URLs and SHA-256 checksums are pinned in
Tooling/export/douyin_template.json in the source workspace.
The ByteDance launcher is distributed unchanged from the official SDK's
runtime download source. It is third-party platform runtime code.
